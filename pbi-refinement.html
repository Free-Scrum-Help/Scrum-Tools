📋 Definition of Ready

Worth knowing: a formal DoR isn't in the Scrum Guide, and Scrum.org calls it "training wheels for a junior team — or an anti-pattern" when it becomes a gate the PO must pass. Treat this as a shared prompt for conversation, never a barrier to starting work.
